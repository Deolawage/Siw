Brief On Siwes Training

I'm Delight Ageloye, a third-year Computer Science student at TASUED and a self-taught frontend developer working toward full-stack capability through The Odin Project. My SIWES placement sits on a Website Development track, giving me hands-on exposure to deployment, debugging, and Coding frontend work alongside my coursework.

Outside the placement, I run Mardel, a film and comics company I co-founded with my brother, and freelance in web development and graphic design. That background shapes how I approach this work attentive to detail, comfortable owning a project end to end, and used to shipping things that real people use.
